How we're covering it

Every match, every day, on the board. From June 11 the main board gets a World Cup tab — each match ranked by confidence with a full three-way moneyline breakdown and a total breakdown.

The draw is the blind spot. A third of group-stage matches historically end level, and casual money never prices it. Our match breakdowns treat the draw as a first-class outcome — including when draw-no-bet or double-chance is the smarter shape.

Honesty about the market: World Cup main lines are the sharpest in all of sports. Our reads run paper-only through the group stage while we measure them against the toughest competition there is. Where soft spots actually live: rotation news on clinched teams, heat and altitude (the Azteca sits at 7,200 feet), dead-rubber third games, and prop markets.

Futures watch

To lift the trophy — no clear king

Prediction-market %: Kalshi via Squawka, 5 Jun 2026. Sportsbook prices: FanDuel, 2–3 Jun 2026.

Spain & France — pick ’em at the top (~16.5% each · +475 / +500). The two best teams on the planet priced as a coin flip. No real value backing either at ~6/1, but it sets the anchor: the true favorite line is a 50/50, worth remembering when their group-winner and match prices land.

England, the lone third pick (10–13% · +650). The only other double-digit side — the market’s clearest talent-vs-temperament bet. Squads this good have flattered to deceive before; the price already assumes they fix it this time.

Argentina, holders, as the value angle (~9% · +900). A Messi farewell, a side that wins tight knockout games, and a kinder draw than the co-favorites — the best price among genuine contenders. The risk is age across a month of North-American heat.

Golden Boot: Mbappé the chalk (+600), Kane next (+700). FOX Sports / RotoWire, early Jun 2026. Mbappé takes France’s penalties and should play deepest of any elite scorer. Haaland (+1400) is the lottery ticket — elite finisher, but Norway likely bow out early.

Hosts are longshots — USA & Mexico ~2% each, Canada ~1% (Kalshi, 5 Jun): all three combined still price below France alone.

Group Bmid

The most open Pot-1 group — Switzerland may be the strongest side here, with Canada, Bosnia and Qatar all live for second.

Group Ctough

Brazil · Morocco · Scotland · Haiti

Group-of-death feel: Brazil and 2022 semifinalist Morocco both fancied through, leaving a returning Scotland and Haiti to scrap for a third-place ticket.

Group Dmid

United States · Paraguay · Australia · Türkiye

The hosts’ draw has bite — Türkiye are dangerous, Paraguay stubborn, Australia organized; nothing is handed to the USA.

Group Esoft

Germany should cruise; Ecuador and Ivory Coast contest second, with debutants Curaçao the minnows.

Group Fmid

Balanced and competitive — the Netherlands favored, but Japan and Sweden make second a genuine fight.

Group Gmid

Belgium ahead of the pack; Salah’s Egypt and a savvy Iran chase second, New Zealand the outsiders.

Group Htough

Brutal — Spain favored, but Uruguay is a heavyweight for a third seed; Cape Verde and Saudi Arabia face a steep climb.

Group Imid

Group Jsoft

Group Ktough
