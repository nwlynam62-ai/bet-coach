Fantasy advice from the sharpest source there is: the betting market.

Rankings sites guess. The market puts money on it. This section reads Vegas win totals, team totals, and player prop markets to find the fantasy values the consensus is sleeping on — and the landmines it's still drafting too high.

How this works

1. Win totals set the table. Offenses attached to high win totals score more, lead more, and feed more fantasy points. We start every team outlook from the number, not the narrative.

2. Props are projections with money behind them. When sportsbooks post a player's yardage and TD props, that's a professional projection you can compare straight against his draft cost.

3. ADP vs the market is the edge. When drafters price a player far below what the prop market implies — that's a pick. Far above — that's a fade. We'll publish both lists all draft season.

Why tiers, not rankings

The best draft tools (FantasyPros' Draft Wizard is the industry standard) all converge on the same insight: drafters don't need rank #23 vs #24 — they need to know where the cliffs are. Tiers tell you when to reach and when to wait. Ours differ in one way: the tier breaks come from betting-market numbers with real money behind them, not staff consensus.
