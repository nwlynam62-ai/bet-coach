2026 Draft Prep · June Edition · Live

Fantasy advice from the sharpest source there is: the betting market.

Rankings sites guess. The market puts money on it. This section reads Vegas win totals, team totals, and player prop markets to find the fantasy values the consensus is sleeping on — and the landmines it's still drafting too high.

How this works

1. Win totals set the table. Offenses attached to high win totals score more, lead more, and feed more fantasy points. We start every team outlook from the number, not the narrative.

2. Props are projections with money behind them. When sportsbooks post a player's yardage and TD props, that's a professional projection you can compare straight against his draft cost.

3. ADP vs the market is the edge. When drafters price a player far below what the prop market implies — that's a pick. Far above — that's a fade. We'll publish both lists all draft season.

The WFL lens — 13-team · full PPR · 6-pt pass TD

Format corrections every public board gets wrong for your league

1. Every market ADP here is half-PPR (Underdog). Your league pays full point per catch: bump high-catch players up — Reed, McConkey, Wan'Dale-profile slots, pass-catching RBs, 100-catch TEs (Bowers, McBride) are all worth MORE than the boards above show.

2. 6-pt passing TDs: QBs are worth more than any public board says. The spread between elite QBs widens (a 40-TD season gains +60–80 pts on the field vs 4-pt scoring), and pocket volume passers gain the most relative value — Burrow and Mahomes are the format's biggest winners. The rushing-QB edge shrinks (a rush TD beats a pass TD by 1.5 pts here, not 3.5) — Lamar stays elite, but the gap behind him closes.

3. 13 teams: scarcity bites. ADP 100–150 players are starters in your league. TE13 is already replacement level — top-3 TE at value, or punt and churn; there is no middle. Roughly 2 QBs rostered per team means QB2s vanish — which is exactly why room-faller Mahomes at 133 is the format steal.

4. Six bench spots, no IR: no handcuffs. The only peer-reviewed test (1,350 leagues) found handcuffing gives ZERO win-rate benefit (51.0% vs 50.6%). Bench = upside swings on 10.5+ win-total offenses. K/DST in the last two rounds, always — CBS autopilot teams take theirs in rounds 9–11 and donate skill depth to you.

Why tiers, not rankings

The best draft tools (FantasyPros' Draft Wizard is the industry standard) all converge on the same insight: drafters don't need rank #23 vs #24 — they need to know where the cliffs are. Tiers tell you when to reach and when to wait. Ours differ in one way: the tier breaks come from betting-market numbers with real money behind them, not staff consensus.
