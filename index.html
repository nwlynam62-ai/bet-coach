How This Works

The board — today's games, ranked, each with my favorite play and a grade. Built from DraftKings lines, refreshed every morning.

Refreshing — tap the Refresh button on the board. The app is a local file so it can't fetch odds itself; refreshing means I re-pull current DraftKings lines and re-grade the slate.

Logging a bet — send me a screenshot of your bet slip in chat. I read it, grade the bet, and add it to your log.

Settling a bet — tell me the result when the game ends and I'll mark it W/L/P.

Grades — I grade the bet: A = clear edge or value, B = solid, C = thin/playable, D = weak, F = a bet to avoid. A trailing + or − (e.g. B+, C−) fine-tunes the call within a tier.

Betting Rules

1. Standard bet = 1 unit. Strong lean = 2u. Max = 3u.

2. Never chase losses by increasing unit size.

3. Walk away after 3 straight losses to reset.

4. Daily stop-loss: −5u  ·  stop-win: +10u (optional lock).

5. Re-evaluate unit size when bankroll moves ±25%.

6. Bet the board as a guide, not gospel — you place the bets you believe in.
